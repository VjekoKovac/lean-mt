# Read the proof of Theorem 1.3

The target is a lower bound for the local Mizohata–Takeuchi constant. For each $0<β≤1$ there must be one $α_β>0$ that works for every positive dimension $d$ and every $C^{2,β}$ hypersurface $Σ⊂ℝ^{d+1}$ with somewhere nonzero second fundamental form. For each surface there must be $c_Σ>0$ and $R_Σ≥1$ with $\mathcal C_Σ(R)≥c_ΣR^{α_β}$ for every real $R≥R_Σ$. Read the definition `MT.PowerLoss` first. In Lean, each `∀` means “for every” and each `∃` means “there exists.” Their order says which constants may depend on earlier choices. [Statement guide](statement.md) · [arXiv v1, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.22465v1#S1)

Let $P(a)=(a,a^2)$. The energy $E(A,B)$ counts ordered pairs $(a,z),(a',z')$ in $A×B$ satisfying $P(a)+z=P(a')+z'$. At scale $H$ a configuration consists of finite nonempty $A⊂[-1,1]$, $B⊂[-1,1]^2$, pairwise separation $1/H$ in $A$, and at most $L$ points of $B$ in every strip $τ-2sb∈I$ of vertical width $1/H$. The gain is $E(A,B)/(L\,\#A\,\#B)$. These are `MT.energy`, `MT.Configuration`, and `MT.Configuration.gain`.

## Section 4 constructs the configurations

Here $k=[K:ℚ]$ is a **field degree**, independent of the hypersurface dimension $d$. Fix a bound $D$ on root discriminants. A loss $b(D)^k$ is allowed with $b(D)$ independent of the later seed size $m$, dilation $ρ$, and degree $k$. Below, $X\lessapprox Y$ means $X≤b(D)^kY$ and $X\gtrapprox Y$ means $Y\lessapprox X$. ## Section 5 fills in the radii

At degrees $k,2k$, the analytic and arithmetic propositions give sample radii $κC^{(1+2/β)k}$ and $κC^{2(1+2/β)k}$. Bracket each sufficiently large **real** radius between consecutive samples. Monotonicity then yields Theorem 1.3 with $α_β=\log(2)/(2(1+2/β)\log C)$. The checked declarations are `MT.localConstant_mono`, `MT.powerLoss_of_dyadic_samples`, and `MT.powerLoss_of_transfer_and_amplification`. [Final deduction](../Solution.lean)
